Argus Lens

Hover over an image on Instagram, Reddit or YouTube and click Check, or right-click any image on any site and choose Check with Argus Lens.

Each image that comes on screen on Instagram, Reddit or YouTube is checked once, and its verdict appears on it. Off by default: every check sends the image to your server and uses API credits.

Images you check are sent to this server, which asks Meta's Muse Spark and SAM 3.1. With automatic checks off, nothing is checked unless you click.
